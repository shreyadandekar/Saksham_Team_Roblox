Key Statutory Capabilities

Core Services & System Functional Modules

Operational modules governed by AICTE Approval Bureau and Ministry of Education oversight standards.

account_balance 1. Central Deposit Registry

National ledger cataloging FDR instruments across scheduled commercial banks with automated principal, tenure, and branch verification records.

Mandate: APH Section 3.2
domain_verification 2. Tripartite Lien Endorsement

Branch IFSC authentication with the Reserve Bank of India directory to validate tripartite pledge terms and authorized bank signatory declarations.

Directory: RBI Empanelled
document_scanner 3. Scrutiny & Document Audit

Council desk examination of FDR physical receipts, seal authenticity, term tenures, and systematic deficiency notices for shortfall rectifications.

Desk TAT: 24-48 Hours
alarm 4. Maturity Monitoring (T-90)

Automated institutional notifications at 90, 60, and 30-day intervals prior to FDR expiration to ensure mandatory rollover without regulatory lapse.

Escalation: T-90 / T-60 / T-30
verified_user 5. Multi-Desk Clearances

Sequential signoffs connecting Finance Bureau, Legal Cell, and Competent Authority with Aadhaar / e-Sign authenticated clearances.

Tiers: Finance • Legal • CA
lock_open 6. Lien Revocation & e-NOC

Release of No-Objection Certificate (e-NOC) upon completion of statutory tenure with encrypted QR authentication for bank dispatch.

Verification: QR-Code Sealed
receipt_long 7. Cumulative Interest Audits

Periodic verification of compounded interest accrued on the fixed deposit as stipulated under the original AICTE sanction memorandum.

Integration: PFMS Portal
history_edu 8. Immutable Audit Trail

Time-stamped transaction logs compliant with Right to Information (RTI Act 2005) and Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) inspection norms.

Audit: CVC / RTI Ready
Procedural Guidelines

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for FDR Lifecycle Management

Six-stage regulatory compliance workflow as mandated in AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH 2026-27).

चरण / STEP 1 Stage 1
Intake & Submission

Institute registers tripartite FDR receipt, IFSC code, and tenure certificate on portal.

Action: Institution Nodal
चरण / STEP 2 Stage 2
Bank Branch Audit

Verification against scheduled bank directory for branch authority and lien confirmation.

Action: Bank / System
चरण / STEP 3 Stage 3
Scrutiny Desk Vetting

AICTE scrutiny officer inspects document clauses, margin marks, and marks deficiencies if any.

Action: Scrutiny Officer
चरण / STEP 4 Stage 4
Finance & Legal Sanction

Formal clearance from AICTE Finance Division and Legal Cell endorsing tripartite compliance.

Action: Internal Bureau
चरण / STEP 5 Stage 5
Active Escrow & Monitoring

Deposit held under active lien. Automated notices sent at T-90, T-60, and T-30 days before maturity.

Action: Monitoring Engine
चरण / STEP 6 Stage 6
e-NOC / Lien Discharge

Upon successful fulfillment of term, digitally signed electronic NOC generated for the bank.

Action: Competent Authority
